2026-08-27

Counter numbers for Mega raids add up now

  • Damage and survival estimates against a Mega boss were worked out from its ordinary form, which made every figure read about a third too high. They now use the Mega's own defence

Current Raids now ends bosses when they really end

  • Raid bosses are now tied to the event schedule, so one drops off the page when its rotation actually finishes instead of lingering for days
  • A new boss shows up as soon as its rotation begins, and Shadow Raids are covered too
  • Mega raids too: a new Mega appears the moment its rotation starts, carrying its own typing and CP rather than its base form's
  • Every boss now tells you how long it has left on your own clock, and during a changeover it says whether it is only just starting or still running in later time zones
  • A new "Up next" strip shows the rotation coming after this one

Writing in your own script no longer breaks a report

  • A long player report written in Japanese, Thai, Hindi or with emoji could fail to send, with an error you could not get past by trying again. It goes through now
  • The same fault reached the translator application, custom pronouns, your favourite Pokémon and the comment left when you rate a bug report. Every field of that kind now counts its length in characters, so nothing is ever cut in the middle of a letter

Applying to translate works whatever you speak

  • Writing your reasons in Japanese, Thai or Hindi could be turned away as the wrong length when it was comfortably inside the limit. The count matches what the form asks for now
  • Ticking a long list of languages, or adding one of your own, could fail the application outright with nothing on screen to explain it. There is room for every language on offer

More of the companion app stops needing the website

  • Bug and support threads now work in the app: opening one, reading the replies, answering, inviting someone in and rating the outcome. Until now the app could file a report but never see what came back
  • Following, blocking and leaving feedback on a trainer work from the app too, as does granting a community award
  • Supporter tag controls are available as well: requesting a tag, changing its colour and cancelling. Buying one still happens on the website

Settings, trainers and signing up, all native in the app

  • Your trainer name, friend code, avatar, pronouns, location and favourite Pokémon can now be set in the app itself. The raid finder needs a name and a code before it will let you in, and until now that meant being sent out to a web page
  • The trainer directory, a trainer's profile and their friends list are all available in the app too
  • Signing up and asking for a password reset work in the app, so a new trainer no longer starts on a web page inside the app
  • A profile marked private now stays private everywhere: the app is told nothing but the username, in the same way the website shows nothing but the username
2026-08-26

Costumes in the app, picked instead of typed

  • The companion app can now show you every costume a Pokémon can wear, each with its shiny picture, so you no longer have to know the exact name and type it
  • The names the game uses are matched too, so searching for "Professor Willow's Assistant" finds "Willow's Lab Coat"
  • Delibird's winter costume was listed twice under two names. It is now listed once, as "Holiday Outfit", the same name its family already uses, and anything recorded under the old name still shows its art

Report a player from the app

  • Reporting a player used to work only on the website. The companion app can now file one too, and it lands in the same queue for staff

Raid counters were naming the wrong Pokémon

  • The counter list under every raid boss was in the wrong order, and several of the strongest attackers were missing from it completely. Against Shadow Slowpoke it opened Regieleki, Gengar, Arcanine when the truth is Regieleki, Zacian, Deoxys, and Zacian, Deoxys, Xurkitree, Darkrai and Kartana never appeared at all
  • The cause was Ditto. Its only fast move never charges an attack, so its score came out as not a number, and that one value was enough to unsettle the ordering of the whole table for every boss
  • The Compare tab on the DPS page and the scoring of your own box against a boss went wrong the same way whenever a Ditto was in the mix. All of them are correct now

The app can see which sections are switched off

  • New GET /api/mobile/v1/maintenance tells the companion app which pages and sections are enabled, the store included, so it can grey out anything the site has turned off instead of opening a screen that will not load
  • It needs no login, which matches the website, where a switched off section is already missing from the menu

Site links can open in the Android app

  • The site now publishes the file Android needs before it will let the companion app open pogo.hails.app links. Tapping a raid finder, events or shiny dex link from a chat, an email or a search result can go straight to the app instead of the browser
  • Only applies to app builds signed with a listed key, and Android checks it when the app is installed, so an existing install needs a reinstall before it takes effect

Event listings cleaned up

  • Event names with an ampersand in them read properly again, on the card, in the details panel and in the calendar feed.
  • Reward, item and Pokemon pictures are back to a sensible size. They were drawing at full size and sprawling over the text beside them.
  • The small species badge on a GO Pass reward no longer covers the icon it sits on, and item counts sit on the corner instead of across the middle.
  • An event no longer repeats its own name and type at the top of its details.
  • GO Pass events listed their rewards twice. Only the rank table remains, with Basic and Deluxe lined up properly.
  • Footnotes read as small print again instead of as bold headings.
  • Research steps, claim steps and the GO Pass table are laid out properly, and event details now fit a phone screen.
  • The LEGO event's store finder was arriving as unreadable text, so it is gone. The link to the official map stays.
  • A GO Pass with no rewards announced yet is left out instead of drawing an empty panel.
  • Events no longer open with a stack of blank lines.
2026-08-25

Seven costumes added, five of which the site could not see at all

  • Two costumes from this month can now be recorded: World Championships 2026 and PokémonXP, both on Pikachu. The admin panel had already spotted these and asked for an update, which is exactly what it is there for
  • The other five were a different problem. Part of how the site decides what counts as a costume comes from a list kept by the game's data community, and that list had simply not marked them. A costume nobody marks is invisible twice over: it never gets added, and the check that looks for missing costumes cannot see it either, so nothing anywhere says it is missing. If you caught one you got a plain shiny with no costume art and no way to record it
  • Those five are in now: the anniversary Pikachu wigs from the Malaysia, Singapore, Philippines and Taiwan events, and the Psyduck sitting in a swim ring
  • To stop it happening again, the site now finds that gap by itself. Costumes from an event carry the year in their internal name, and permanent forms like Mega and Alolan never do, which turns out to be enough to tell the two apart. Anything that looks like an event costume but that the game will not confirm is now listed for a person to look at, rather than dropped in silence
  • It still will not add any of them on its own. A costume becomes recordable only once someone has looked at the artwork and given it a name, which is unchanged and deliberate: the game's internal names mislead often enough that guessing from them has put the wrong artwork on a costume before
  • Nothing you have already recorded is affected, and the two Ditto caps are still waiting on a name
2026-08-05

The costume check now notices costumes it used to miss

  • Costumes are read straight out of the game's own files, so the site knows which ones exist, which Pokémon can wear each one, and whether the shiny version was ever made. A check in the admin panel asks whether the game has added any we do not have yet. It was answering the wrong question
  • It only ever looked for brand new costumes. When an existing costume was later given to more Pokémon, which is how most events extend one, it saw nothing to report, and anyone who caught the costume on one of the new species got a plain shiny with no costume art. It now spots that too, and names the species that were added
  • It could also have nagged forever about a costume that only exists in the female version of a sprite, asking for an update that would never have changed anything
  • The check counted every file in the game's art folder and then described the total as costumes. It now says how many costume sprites it actually looked at
  • Two presses of the button in quick succession no longer means two trips to the game files. The answer is held for a few minutes and pressing again asks for a fresh look, which stops the site running out of its allowance and reporting a limit as though the source had gone missing
  • The tool that rebuilds costume data used to report itself as out of date whenever the game's art repository received any commit at all, even one that touched nothing about costumes. It now separates a real costume change from that bookkeeping, so a warning means something again
  • Some costumes are in the game and still have no name on this site, so they cannot be recorded yet. Nothing you have already recorded is affected. They are named by hand on purpose, because the game's internal names are misleading (one called "May 2019" is a straw hat), and the admin panel now says plainly when there is no outside name to check a new one against
  • A costume name given by mistake can now be taken back. Until now a name vanished from the list the instant it was given, so a wrong one could not be reached to correct it. It can only be undone while nobody has recorded that costume, because removing a name people have used would blank the artwork on their entries

Accounts can now be deleted, not only suspended

  • Until now the strongest thing staff could do to an account was suspend it, which locks the trainer out but keeps everything on file. There is now a way to remove an account outright, for spam signups, throwaway test accounts, and anyone who asks for their data to be erased
  • Only the site owner can do it, and it asks for the username to be typed out in full before it will go ahead. There is no undo and no copy kept, so the extra step is the point
  • Deleting an account takes everything belonging to it: the shiny collection, raid history, ratings, reports, messages, purchases and saved devices. The trainer is signed out everywhere the moment it happens
  • Other people's records are left standing. If the deleted account had rated or raided with someone, that trainer's own history and trust score are untouched rather than quietly shifting
  • Strikes now outlive the staff member who issued them. Previously, deleting a moderator would have deleted every strike they had ever given out, wiping the record of the trainers they disciplined. The strike stays, still showing who issued it
  • That last part is a database change. The upgrade tool applies it, and a brand new install needs nothing extra

Your Pokémon now have a home, and the Raids page uses them

  • There is a new My Pokémon page in the menu. It holds the Pokémon you have saved, and you can now add one by hand as well as remove it. Until now the only way to put anything in there was to run an IV check first, or to use the companion app, which has not been released
  • Adding by hand is for a Pokémon whose appraisal you already know: pick it, set its CP, level and IVs, and mark it as shadow or purified. If you do not know the IVs, the IV Calculator still works them out from CP, HP and stardust, and saving from there fills the box as before
  • Open a raid boss on the Raids page and your own Pokémon are now ranked against it, shown first, above the general list of top counters. The best five appear straight away and the rest are one tap behind them, so the question of what to actually bring is answered before the question of what the theoretical best would be
  • Because your box records which Pokémon you own but not which moves they know, each one shows two numbers: what it does on its best moveset and what it does on its worst. A Pokémon whose IVs were never pinned down still appears, marked, using the average of the possibilities rather than pretending it is perfect
  • Shadow Pokémon are counted properly. Shadow hits noticeably harder in a raid, and the box can now record that, which often changes which of your Pokémon is actually the best pick
  • The old "Check your Pokémon" box at the top of the page is unchanged if you liked comparing one Pokémon across every current raid
  • This adds two columns to the database. The upgrade tool handles it, and a brand new install needs nothing extra

Counter damage figures were wrong for most raid bosses

  • Every Shadow, Alolan, Hisuian, Galarian and Mega boss was being scored against a placeholder defence stat instead of its own, because those bosses are named differently from the Pokédex entry the site looked them up in. On a recent rotation that was 14 of the 19 bosses on the board
  • The order of the counters list was never affected, so the advice you were reading was sound. The DPS and TDO numbers next to it were not, and were often out by more than half
  • There were two halves to it. Bosses named with a prefix, like Shadow Slowpoke or Alolan Sandshrew, matched nothing at all and got a placeholder. Bosses named plainly, like Mewtwo or Deoxys, matched the wrong forme instead: Mewtwo was being scored as Armored Mewtwo and Deoxys as its Attack forme, which are not the ones you fight. Both are right now, and a check runs against the live boss list so it cannot drift back quietly
  • One gap remains and is worth being straight about: Mega bosses fall back to the ordinary form's defence, because the data we get does not carry Mega stats at all. That is closer than the old placeholder but it is not exact, and it cannot be until the source provides them

Event details that were quietly missing are back

  • Opening a GO Battle League event showed a blank panel where the details should be. The page was being fetched and stored correctly the whole time, then refused on the way back out because those events are named with underscores and the check that guards them did not allow one. Every League rotation reads properly now
  • Event details were also disappearing partway through an event. The site worked out whether an event had finished using the clock where the server sits, but most events run at the same hour in every timezone, so anything still running in the Americas looked over hours before it was. Those events kept their card and lost their details. It now waits until an event has genuinely finished everywhere on Earth
  • An event with nothing extra to show used to open an empty panel. It now says so plainly instead of looking broken
  • The subscribe list only offered a fixed set of event types, so choosing specific types quietly left out GO Fest, seasons, GO Pass and a few others. Anything the schedule carries now appears in the list on its own, and no type can go missing again
  • If the source we take events from ever publishes something empty or malformed, the site now keeps what it already has instead of throwing it away. Previously one bad update could have wiped every stored event page with nothing to restore from
2026-07-27

See which shinies are coming, and when

  • Niantic usually announces a shiny weeks before it arrives. Those dates can now be recorded, so My Collection tells you what is coming instead of only what is out
  • A Pokemon with an announced shiny shows on the checklist straight away, greyed out with the date in the corner and the full date when you hover it. You still cannot add it, because it does not exist yet. Everything else that is unreleased stays tucked behind the "Show unreleased" tickbox as before
  • The Missing tab is unchanged: it lists shinies you could go out and catch today, so an announced one is not on it
  • On the day, the shiny turns itself on. Nobody has to be awake to tick a box. If Niantic delays a release we can hold it back by hand, and doing so overrules the date
  • Staff can set a date for regional and alternate forms too, not just for a species
  • This adds one column to the database. The upgrade tool handles it, and a brand new install needs nothing extra

Release v0.1.7e

  • Everything in the 25 July entry below is bundled into a tagged release, v0.1.7e, for anyone running their own copy
  • This one does change the database, so upgrading is not just a rebuild and a restart. There is one new table that stores our corrections to the shiny list, and the upgrade tool creates it for you. A brand new install runs the schema and needs nothing extra
  • Build and upgrade instructions live on the project wiki, on the Releases and Upgrading page
2026-07-25

Shiny Solgaleo is here, and the shiny list will not fall behind again

  • Shiny Solgaleo was released in Pokemon GO on 22 July and was missing from My Collection. It is there now. Thank you to the trainer who reported it
  • The real problem was not one missing Pokemon, it was how the list worked. The site took its shiny list from an outside source that stopped being updated earlier this year, and a Pokemon simply being absent from that list was how the site decided its shiny "did not exist yet". So every new shiny release needed a code change and a rebuild, and 141 Pokemon had quietly gone missing
  • My Collection now ships with the entire National Pokedex, all 1025 species, each marked with whether it is in Pokemon GO and whether its shiny has been released. When Niantic releases a shiny, we tick a box and it appears straight away. No more waiting for an update
  • Nothing looks different unless you want it to. The checklist still shows only the shinies you can actually catch, and the Missing tab is still a real to do list. There is a new "Show unreleased" tickbox under the search if you would like to see what is coming: those Pokemon appear greyed out, marked either "soon" or as not being in the game at all, and cannot be added
  • Anything already in your collection always stays visible and editable, even if we have its release status wrong. Your catches are yours
  • Shiny Lunala is announced for 19 August. It will appear on the day, without an update

Paldean Tauros, all three of them

  • Paldean Tauros comes in three breeds, Combat, Blaze and Aqua, and each has its own shiny. We were listing it as a single Pokemon whose shiny was not out yet, because our sources disagreed about it. They agree now, so all three breeds are on the checklist as separate cards with their own artwork
  • Two Vivillon patterns have gone the other way. Fancy and Poke Ball are event exclusive and their shinies have never actually been catchable, so they no longer sit on the checklist pretending otherwise. Tick "Show unreleased" if you would like to see them waiting
  • As always, anything already in your collection is untouched and still yours to edit

A faster checklist, and evolutions that do not wait for us

  • Typing in the collection search used to redraw the whole page on every single key press, and worked out whether each of a thousand cards was caught by reading through your entire collection again each time. Searching is now near instant even with a very full collection
  • The Evolve button now offers any Pokemon that exists in Pokemon GO, rather than only ones whose shiny we have already listed. If you evolve a shiny into something the day its evolution becomes possible, you no longer have to wait for us to catch up
  • The plain version of a Pokemon in the add pop up no longer shows a broken image icon when no artwork exists for it

Costume suggestions now work on a phone

  • The Costume box in the add and edit pop ups used to lean on a browser feature that quietly does nothing on most phones. On a computer it suggested costumes as you typed; on a phone it suggested nothing at all, so unless you already knew exactly what a costume was called, there was no way to find out it existed
  • Tap the Costume box now, before typing anything, and you get a scrollable list of every costume that Pokemon can wear, each with its own shiny picture. Pick one by looking at it rather than by guessing its name
  • You can still type anything you like in there. A costume the site has not named yet is saved exactly as you write it, same as before
  • Thank you to the trainer who reported Professor Willow's assistant Pikachu as missing. It was not missing, it was invisible, which is arguably worse

Searching a costume by the name the game uses

  • We call the Willow birthday costume "Willow's Lab Coat". Every event page calls it "Professor Willow's assistant". Typing either one now finds it, and the search also looks inside the middle of a name, so "lab coat" works too
  • Existing entries are untouched. Nothing was renamed, because renaming a costume would blank the picture on catches people have already saved

The admin costume check tells the truth again

  • The button that asks the game's mined files whether there are costumes we have not picked up yet had a blind spot: it only recognised one of the two ways a costume can be filed, and every costume released since roughly 2024 uses the other one. It answered "nothing new" no matter what had come out
  • It now applies the same test the sync tool does, so it can see modern costumes while still ignoring the regional, Mega and Gigantamax forms that are not costumes at all
  • Its answer also used to be wiped off the screen a moment after appearing, which made a working check look like a dead button. The result now stays put, and it says "no new codes upstream" out loud instead of going quiet
2026-07-18

My Collection has a new look

  • All four tabs now use the same grid of cards, so the page reads as one thing instead of two
  • Caught and Evolved cards have been redesigned. Each shiny sits on a lit pedestal, with its catch method, date, and any costume or event shown as tags
  • Tap a card to edit that entry in a small pop up

The drifting starfield, now matching hails.cc

  • The little specks floating behind every page have been retuned to match the ones on the hails.cc front page. There are more of them, they are brighter, they drift a touch faster and they are small five sided shapes rather than plain dots
  • They still sit behind everything, just above the background picture, and they still scatter away from your cursor as you move it around
