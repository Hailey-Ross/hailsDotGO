Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026. This describes what FlexDex actually does with your data, on the website and in the Android app. FlexDex is a hobby project run by one person, and this page is written to match the code rather than to cover every eventuality.

Questions, or anything you want changed or deleted: flexdex@hails.app.

FlexDex is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from them.

The IV scanner captures your screen

This is the part worth reading. When you use the scanner in the Android app, it captures one frame of your entire screen and reads it on your phone.

The frame is the whole screen, not a crop of the Pokemon. Anything else visible at that moment, such as a notification banner or a message preview, is part of the image while it is being read.

The image never leaves your phone: it is read on the device and released straight away, and release builds of the app never write it to storage. What is sent to this server is only the reading, as numbers and names: the species and form, CP, HP, stardust cost, your trainer level, the appraisal, whether it is lucky, shadow or purified, and the level arc. The server uses them to work out the IVs. These readings are not written to the server log.

On the website, the IV calculator can read a screenshot you upload. That image is sent to this server, read in memory and not stored anywhere afterwards; the values read from it (species, CP, trainer level and similar) are written to the server log.

Your account

An account stores a username, an email address, and a password kept only as a bcrypt hash. Optionally it stores a trainer name, friend code, trainer level, pronouns, avatar, favorite Pokemon, and a city, region and country if you choose to enter them.

You control how much of that is public. The location display setting can hide your location entirely, show only your country, or show your city and region. A private profile hides your trainer name and friend code from other trainers.

Anything you save yourself, such as your Pokemon box, shiny collection, raid history and event reminders, is stored against your account. Event reminders set in the app also store your phone's time zone (for example America/Denver), so a reminder fires at the right local time.

Trades and messages

Willing to Trade conversations use ready made messages only; nothing you type is stored. The conversation is stored on this server and visible to the two trainers in it, and to staff when a report points at it. A conversation with no messages for 14 days is hidden from both trainers, kept for staff for a year, and then deleted unless a report still points at it.

The app

Signing in stores a session token on your phone in encrypted storage. Sessions last up to 30 days and end when you sign out.

If you allow notifications, the app registers a Firebase Cloud Messaging token with this server so it can tell you about raid lobbies and event reminders. Google processes that token to deliver the notifications, and Firebase keeps an installation id for the app. The token is stored here with the device model name, and is removed when you sign out.

Usage data and crash reports (app)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to see which screens are used and how scans go: screen views (by screen type, not by what is on them) and scan steps. It does not send your username, does not use the advertising id, and ad personalization is off. Usage data is on by default; you can turn it off in the app's Settings, Share usage data.

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes: the device model, Android version, app version, what the app was doing (the stack trace), and a few app details such as the species that was being scanned. Reports of crashes are still sent when usage data is off; the extra details and reports of problems that did not crash the app stop.

Usage data from the app (Google Analytics for Firebase) is kept for 2 months at the event level and 14 months at the user level, then deleted; Google may keep anonymous aggregate totals. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are kept for 90 days. You can turn usage data off in the app's Settings, Share usage data.

Google is the processor for both. See Google's privacy policy and Firebase's privacy information.

The Android beta

While the app is in open beta it is shared through Firebase App Distribution. Joining gives Google your email address and your device's details (its name, manufacturer, model and Android version) so it can send you test versions. How to join and how to leave is on the Playtest page.

Other trainers

Raid lobbies, trainer feedback, player reports and bug report threads are visible to the people involved, and to site staff who review them. Reports are read by a human.

Third parties

There is no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Apart from Google's Firebase services in the app (above), these services see a little of your data:

  • Resend sends this site's emails (confirming your address, resetting your password), so it receives your email address and the message.
  • Open-Meteo is asked for the weather where you are: the server sends it the city you entered (or its coordinates), nothing else about you.
  • Your browser loads Pokedex details from PokeAPI and event artwork from LeekDuck. Requesting them tells those hosts your IP address, as any image or request on any website does. Pokemon pictures are served from this site itself.
  • Donations are not set up yet. If they are, PayPal will handle them and receive what it needs to take the payment; this site never sees card details.

How long things are kept

  • Your account and what you save: until you delete it (see below).
  • Sessions: up to 30 days, and they end when you sign out. Push tokens: until you sign out or delete the account.
  • The server log records each request with its IP address, plus the values read from website screenshots. It is kept for a few months and then rotated away.
  • App usage data and crash reports: as above (2 and 14 months; 90 days).
  • Trade conversations: as above (hidden after 14 idle days, deleted after a year).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself: on this website in Settings, Delete account, or in the app in Settings, Delete account. If you can no longer sign in, email flexdex@hails.app from the address on the account. The Delete your account page explains every way and exactly what happens.

A deleted account leaves the site immediately: it stops appearing anywhere, you are signed out everywhere, and open trades end. The record underneath is held for 90 days so a mistake can be fixed; signing in again during that time cancels the deletion. After 90 days it is erased for good. If you would rather it were erased right away, say so by email and it is done that way.

Reports and staff moderation records are kept without your name, so staff history stays complete. Trade conversations keep only the name you had at the time.
